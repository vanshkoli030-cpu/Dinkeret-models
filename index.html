🤖 1. AI-style Robot Builder offline rules

Describe your project in plain language. The local helper picks a starter design and places parts in the 3D workspace. This is a rule-based assistant, not a cloud AI service.

Try a project idea above. Your current workspace is kept until you choose to replace it.
🔌 2. Real Pin-to-Pin Wiring Planner

Choose parts, specify pin names and wire purpose. The wire is drawn in 3D. Compatibility checks are educational warnings, not an electrical certification.

Add two parts to the 3D workspace first.
🧰 3. Enhanced 3D Electronics Library

Your original generated 3D models stay in the workbench. Use category search and this expanded parts picker to add frequently used modules quickly. Models are illustrative, not manufacturer CAD.

Includes controllers, power, sensors, displays, motors, drivers, passive components, wheels, chassis, and wiring from the original library.
⚡ 4. Circuit Logic Simulator

A beginner-friendly DC/LED calculator with simplified Ohm’s-law checks. It does not simulate full circuits, inductive loads, or component thermal behavior.

Press “Run circuit check” to calculate approximate current.
💻 5. Arduino Code Studio
Review pin mapping and the exact module datasheet before uploading. Some sketches require a library or model-specific pin changes.
🧲 6. Snap-Together Assembly

Auto-arrange components on a clean grid or build common kits. It snaps part positions to the workbench grid and supports repeatable layouts.

Add components, then choose Arrange workspace to tidy the layout.
🎮 7. Physics-Inspired Robot Playground

A safe 2D motion sandbox for learning drive commands. It visualizes approximate movement and turning; it is not a physical engine or a prediction of a real robot.

Robot ready. Use the controls to test movement and turning.
🎓 8. Robotics Academy

Short guided lessons and a knowledge check. Progress is stored in this browser.

Why should a motor usually not be connected directly to a microcontroller GPIO pin?
🧾 9. Parts Finder & Budget Planner

Editable rough prices in INR help plan a school project. These are placeholders you can replace with current local prices; not live store prices.

Edit quantities and prices, then recalculate.
🌍 10. Real-World Build Bridge

Turn the digital plan into a build checklist: bill of materials, connection schedule, safety checks, test steps and code export.

Add parts and wiring to create your real-world build checklist.
Before powering hardware: verify the exact board pinout, voltage levels, motor-driver ratings, battery polarity, current limits, and common ground. Do not connect mains voltage in this beginner workspace.
