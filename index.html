🧪 Engineering assessment

Audit checklist

1 · Stability: syntax, missing references, runtime console and startup failures.

2 · UI: selection, placement, movement, rotation, delete, save/load and import/export.

3 · Hardware accuracy: datasheet-grounded pin maps and valid connections for each modeled part.

4 · Circuit simulation: voltage, current, polarity, open circuits and short-circuit checks. Current engine is educational/visual, not a SPICE solver.

5 · 3D quality: models, materials, lighting, shadows, animation and responsive performance.

6 · Regression: repeat all checks on desktop and mobile.

Status: Checklist added; these are not marked passed unless a test has actually been run.
